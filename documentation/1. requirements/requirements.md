# Requirements

## What it must do

1. Put a council or committee **meeting** on record: its name, the day it is
   held, where, and which committee holds it.
2. Hold one **agenda** and one set of **minutes** per meeting, each replaced as
   a whole and refused when the save was made on an old version.
3. Put a **resolution** on record: its title, the day, its outcome and, when
   there is one, the meeting that took it; list and read resolutions.
4. Put a **bylaw** on record: its title, the year it was made and its text.
5. Correct a meeting, a resolution or a bylaw after the fact; a resolution
   cannot be moved to another meeting by an edit.
6. Keep every agency's council apart: every read is narrowed to one instance.

## Out of scope

Who may see or change a council record. The mounting host resolves the caller
and the role's permissions; this module only names the actions
(`council.<object>.<verb>`).
